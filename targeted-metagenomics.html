Targeted Metagenomics (16S / ITS) 30-day workshop

Profile which microbes are present and how communities differ.

About this workshop

Targeted metagenomics identifies the microbes in a sample by sequencing one marker gene, the 16S rRNA gene for bacteria and archaea or the ITS region for fungi. It is a common first step in microbiome studies of gut, soil, water and plant samples. This workshop shows how to turn amplicon reads into results: removing primers, denoising reads into exact sequence variants, removing chimeras and assigning taxonomy against a reference database. You then describe diversity within and between samples, make ordination plots and test which taxa differ between groups, with attention to the pitfalls of compositional data and unequal sequencing depth. You also learn to predict likely community functions from the marker data and to present the results clearly.

Weeks 2-3

Weeks 3-4

Beta diversity Compare communities between samples.

Predicted function Infer pathways from marker genes.

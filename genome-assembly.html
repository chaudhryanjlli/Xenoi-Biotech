Whole-Genome de novo Assembly 30-day workshop

Assemble a genome from sequencing reads and describe its genes.

About this workshop

Assembling a genome means reconstructing a full sequence from millions of short or long fragments, with no reference to guide you. Quality depends on decisions made before assembly starts, such as how large and repetitive the genome is and which reads to use. This workshop begins with estimating genome size and complexity from the reads, then compares short-read, long-read and combined strategies. You learn how to polish an assembly, judge it with statistics and completeness checks, and remove contamination and duplicated sequence. The second half covers annotation: masking repeats, predicting genes and assigning functions to them. By the end you will have taken a small genome from raw reads to an annotated assembly and will know how to judge the quality of other people's.

What you will learn

Module 1 Foundations
Week 1
Linux and command-line basics Run tools and plan storage.
Genome assembly concepts Reads, contigs, scaffolds and chromosomes.
Sequencing technologies Short reads, long reads and when to combine them.
Read quality control Evaluate and clean reads.
Module 2 Genome characteristics
Weeks 2-3
Genome size estimation Estimate size and complexity from k-mers.
Heterozygosity and repeats Know what to expect before assembly.
Resource planning Estimate memory and time needs.
Read preparation Select and correct reads for assembly.
Module 3 Assembly
Weeks 2-3
Short-read assembly Build contigs from short reads.
Long-read assembly Build longer, more contiguous sequences.
Hybrid approaches Combine read types.
Comparing assemblies Test different tools and settings.
Module 4 Polishing and quality
Weeks 3-4
Error correction Polish the assembly with reads.
Assembly statistics Interpret N50 and other metrics.
Completeness checks Test for expected core genes.
Contamination and duplicates Find and remove unwanted sequence.
Module 5 Annotation
Weeks 3-4
Repeat identification Find and mask repeats.
Gene prediction Predict genes with evidence and models.
Functional annotation Assign names, domains and GO terms.
Annotation summary Report gene counts and features.
Module 6 Mini-project and report
Week 4
Project dataset Assemble and annotate a small genome.
Reproducible workflow Record every step and setting.
Figures and write-up Present assembly and annotation results.
Review and next steps Discuss scaffolding and improvement.

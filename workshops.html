Bioinformatics Workshops & Training

18 hands-on NGS workshops, each a 30-day programme with practical modules and a mini-project on real data.

This page outlines Xenoi Biotech's practical bioinformatics training programs and hands-on bootcamps for researchers and students. Our curriculum covers command-line genomics, Python programming for biological datasets, and end-to-end single-cell RNA-seq pipelines. Participants gain practical dry-lab experience with real datasets, complementing our internship programmes and Career Navigator.

Xenoi Biotech runs 18 online NGS workshops covering transcriptomics, epigenomics, genomics, metagenomics and microarray analysis.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information that you voluntarily submit through our project quote and enquiry form. This includes your full name, email address, organisation or institution, service interest, and any details provided in your message. This information is used solely to respond to your enquiry and discuss potential collaboration or training requirements.

2. Form Delivery & Processing

Our enquiry form is processed and delivered by Web3Forms, a third-party form processor, directly to our secure email inbox.

3. Cookies & Browser Storage

This website uses no advertising cookies, no analytics scripts, and no third-party tracking mechanisms.

The site stores only a small frame-position value in your browser's session storage (sessionStorage) to ensure smooth transitions and continuous playback of the DNA scroll animation across page navigations.

4. Data Sharing & Retention

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with any third parties. Enquiry emails are retained only for as long as necessary to address your requests and for standard business record keeping.

5. Your Rights & Access Requests

You may at any time request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by sending an email to explore@xenoibiotech.com. You may also review our general Terms of Use.

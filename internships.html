Bioinformatics and Genomics Internship Programmes

Specialisation tracks — Choose your area of interest

Students can select a track based on their interests and career goals.

NGS & Genomics

NGS data analysis; variant analysis; genome annotation

RNA-seq & Transcriptomics

RNA-seq analysis pipeline; differential expression; functional enrichment

Computational & Structural Biology

Protein structure analysis; molecular modelling; docking and visualisation

AI/ML for Biological Data

Machine-learning basics; predictive modelling; applications in genomics and drug discovery

Reproducible Bioinformatics Workflows

Workflow development (Snakemake/Nextflow); data management and visualisation; reproducible research practices

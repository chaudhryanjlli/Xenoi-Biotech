How to apply

Joining a Xenoi Biotech internship programme is straightforward through our 4-step onboarding process:

  1. Submit application enquiry Fill out our online enquiry form indicating your academic background, preferred duration (4, 8, or 12 weeks), and track of interest.
  2. Short consultation call Our academic mentors arrange a brief discussion to understand your learning objectives, computational setup, and project prerequisites.
  3. Formal offer letter You receive an official internship offer letter specifying your curriculum milestones, project scope, and batch schedule for college approval.
  4. Payment & workspace onboarding Confirm your seat, complete payment, and receive your workspace credentials, dataset access, and introductory orientation materials.

Frequently asked questions

Who is eligible for Xenoi Biotech bioinformatics internships?

Students currently pursuing or completing B.Sc., M.Sc., B.Tech, M.Tech, or Ph.D. degrees in biotechnology, bioinformatics, microbiology, biochemistry, or life sciences are eligible. No prior programming experience is required for the 4-week Foundation level.

Are internships conducted online or in-person?

All Xenoi Biotech internship programmes are conducted 100% online with remote mentor check-ins and cloud compute guidance. Students from anywhere in India (including Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad, and Punjab) and international candidates can participate.

What computational resources or hardware do I need?

A standard laptop or desktop with at least 8 GB RAM and a reliable internet connection is sufficient. For high-performance computing tasks, guidance on cloud or remote server environments is provided.

Will I receive a verified certificate upon completion?

Yes, interns who successfully complete their assigned modules, project tasks, and final project report receive a verified Certificate of Completion from Xenoi Biotech Pvt. Ltd.

Can I use the internship project for my academic thesis or degree requirement?

Yes. Many students utilize the 8-week Applied or 12-week Capstone internship project work and documentation to fulfill their university dissertation, project semester, or summer training requirements.

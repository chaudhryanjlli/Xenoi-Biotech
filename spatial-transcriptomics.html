About this workshop

Conventional expression data tells you what a tissue is making but not where. Spatial transcriptomics keeps the location of each measurement, so you can see how gene activity is organised across a tissue section and how regions relate to the underlying structure. This workshop covers the data from the first step: understanding spots, coordinates and tissue images, filtering low-quality or off-tissue spots, and normalising for depth. You then group spots into regions, find genes whose expression follows spatial patterns, and describe the cell mixtures within each spot using single-cell references. The result is a set of maps and gene lists that link molecular signals to tissue architecture. It is useful for anyone working with tissue sections in development, cancer or neuroscience.

Frequently asked questions

Is this Spatial Transcriptomics workshop delivered online?

Yes. All Xenoi Biotech workshops are delivered 100% online, allowing researchers and students across India and internationally to participate with remote compute support.

How long is the workshop and what is the workload?

The workshop runs for 30 days across 6 structured modules. Learners typically dedicate 1 to 2 flexible hours per day to work through pipeline commands, code exercises, and the mini-project.

Who is this workshop designed for?

It is built for B.Sc., M.Sc., and Ph.D. students, postdoctoral researchers, faculty members, and industry professionals in biotechnology, biochemistry, microbiology, and medicine who need to analyze NGS datasets independently.

What will I learn in this workshop?

You will master Space Ranger output interpretation, tissue image alignment, spot-level QC, spatially variable gene identification with Moran's I and SpatialDE, spot clustering, and cellular deconvolution using single-cell reference datasets.

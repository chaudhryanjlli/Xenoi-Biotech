About this workshop

One gene can produce several different transcripts when its exons are joined in different ways. This alternative splicing helps explain how a limited set of genes gives rise to so much biological variety, and splicing changes are linked to many diseases. In this workshop you learn to detect splicing events from RNA-Seq reads, from mapping across exon junctions to building a catalogue of events such as skipped exons and retained introns. You then calculate percent spliced in, the measure of how often an exon is included, and test whether it changes between conditions. You also learn how to judge when coverage is too low to trust a value, how to visualise splicing, and how to relate a change to the protein domains it affects.

Frequently asked questions

Is this PSI-Seq (alternative splicing) workshop delivered online?

Yes. All Xenoi Biotech workshops are delivered 100% online, allowing researchers and students across India and internationally to participate with remote compute support.

How long is the workshop and what is the workload?

The workshop runs for 30 days across 6 structured modules. Learners typically dedicate 1 to 2 flexible hours per day to work through pipeline commands, code exercises, and the mini-project.

Who is this workshop designed for?

It is built for B.Sc., M.Sc., and Ph.D. students, postdoctoral researchers, faculty members, and industry professionals in biotechnology, biochemistry, microbiology, and medicine who need to analyze NGS datasets independently.

What will I learn in this workshop?

You will learn exon junction alignment in STAR, splice event classification (skipped exon, mutually exclusive exons, retained introns), PSI estimation and differential testing in rMATS and SUPPA2, and sashimi plot generation for publication figures.

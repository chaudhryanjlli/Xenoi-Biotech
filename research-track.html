6-MONTH MENTORED RESEARCH INTERNSHIP

From internship to manuscript

A 6-month mentored research track in bioinformatics, ending in a manuscript we submit for you. Most internships end with a certificate; this one ends with a research paper. You take a real biological question built around public data, such as RNA-seq, single-cell, metagenomics or protein-structure datasets, analyse it with a mentor guiding you at every stage, and write it up. Along the way you learn the tools professionals use: Linux, quality control, alignment, statistics, visualisation and reproducible workflows on GitHub.

By month six you hold a complete manuscript that you understand from start to finish, a code repository that shows how you reached your results, and mentor feedback on every section. When it passes our review, we submit it to a journal on your behalf, handling journal selection and submission, so you can focus on the science.

How the 6 months work

1 Month 1, Foundations

Learn the tools, choose your question and plan the analysis.

2 Months 2 to 3, Analysis

Work through the data with regular mentor reviews.

3 Month 4, Results

Build figures, check findings and test them.

4 Month 5, Writing

Draft the manuscript section by section with feedback.

5 Month 6, Review and submission

Final mentor review, journal selection and submission by Xenoi.

How authorship works

  • ✓Authorship follows real contribution to the work.
  • ✓You are listed as an author for the analysis and writing you do.
  • ✓Your mentor guides the project and reviews the manuscript. Authors are agreed before submission, and every author approves the final version.
  • ✓We choose properly indexed journals, and we declare Xenoi's role in the submission.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know programming?

No. We start from the basics and you learn the tools as you work.

Who submits the paper?

Xenoi submits it, after it has passed mentor review and you have approved the final version.

Whose data do we use?

Public or properly permitted datasets, credited correctly.

About Xenoi Biotech

Research-focused bioinformatics analysis and hands-on training, led by Director Anjali Chaudhary and our international engineering and computational biology team.

Xenoi Biotech Pvt. Ltd. is an Indian bioinformatics and computational biology enterprise based in Punjab, India. We operate at the intersection of high-throughput sequencing data analysis, containerized workflow engineering, and specialized life-science training. Founded to bridge the critical gap between raw instrument outputs and authentic biological discoveries, we deliver production-grade NGS pipelines for research scholars, academic departments, and commercial laboratories in India and internationally.

Our core philosophy centers on scientific reproducibility and transparent methodology. In our commercial services, every client receives validated alignments, audited quality metrics, and manuscript-ready methods. In our educational division, we provide intensive 30-day online workshops and virtual internships that immerse students in Linux environments, terminal workflows, and statistical packages rather than superficial demonstrations.

Why Punjab & India: Advancing Regional Excellence

Punjab and northern India possess a rich heritage of agricultural and biomedical research, spanning premier central universities, ICAR agricultural research institutes, and medical colleges. Establishing Xenoi Biotech in Punjab allows us to cultivate grassroots computational expertise, providing students and research scholars in the region with direct access to global-standard bioinformatics mentorship. Simultaneously, our digital infrastructure enables seamless collaboration with investigators across Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Chennai, and overseas research centers.

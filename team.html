ABOUT US

Our Team

Research-focused bioinformatics analysis and hands-on training, led by our Director and Lead Bioinformatician.

Xenoibiotech is a bioinformatics company based in Punjab, India. We work on next-generation sequencing data analysis, omics data interpretation and biological AI/ML, and we run online bioinformatics workshops and internships for students and researchers across India and internationally.

This page introduces the leadership and scientific team at Xenoi Biotech, based in Punjab, India. We bring together deep computational expertise in genomic data analysis, bioinformatics pipeline architecture, and scalable research infrastructure. We work with researchers and organisations to deliver robust bioinformatics services and practical training. We teach through online bioinformatics workshops and internships.

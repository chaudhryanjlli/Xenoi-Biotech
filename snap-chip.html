About this workshop

Standard ChIP-Seq is good at showing where a histone modification is, but it is poor at showing whether the total amount of that modification has changed between samples. Spike-in calibration solves this by adding a known set of reference nucleosomes, each carrying a defined modification, to every sample. Because the amounts are known, the reads they produce can be used to scale the data and to check how specific the antibody is. This workshop teaches how to count the spike-in reads, calculate scaling factors, build calibrated signal tracks and compare them with standard normalisation. You will see what calibration changes in practice, especially when a treatment alters a mark across the whole genome, and how to report such results with confidence.

Frequently asked questions

Is this SNAP-ChIP workshop delivered online?

Yes. All Xenoi Biotech workshops are delivered 100% online, allowing researchers and students across India and internationally to participate with remote compute support.

How long is the workshop and what is the workload?

The workshop runs for 30 days across 6 structured modules. Learners typically dedicate 1 to 2 flexible hours per day to work through pipeline commands, code exercises, and the mini-project.

Who is this workshop designed for?

It is built for B.Sc., M.Sc., and Ph.D. students, postdoctoral researchers, faculty members, and industry professionals in biotechnology, biochemistry, microbiology, and medicine who need to analyze NGS datasets independently.

What will I learn in this workshop?

You will learn to quantify synthetic spike-in nucleosome barcodes, evaluate antibody specificity on-target vs off-target, compute linear scaling factors, generate calibrated bigWig coverage tracks in deepTools, and test differential global marks.

BIOINFORMATICS & DATA SERVICES

Bioinformatics Analysis Services & Pipelines

Xenoi Biotech provides end-to-end bioinformatics analysis services, custom workflow engineering, and statistical interpretation for academic researchers, biotechnology enterprises, and clinical laboratories worldwide.

Navigating complex next-generation sequencing datasets requires computational precision, robust statistical models, and reproducible pipeline architecture. Based in Punjab, India, our team collaborates with principal investigators and commercial teams to transform raw sequencing reads into validated biological insights. Whether your project involves differential gene expression, chromatin accessibility, variant discovery, or metagenomic profiling, we combine industry-standard tools with transparent reporting so that your findings withstand rigorous peer review. For focused sequencing data solutions, review our dedicated genomics analysis services. Researchers interested in building in-house computational skills can also explore our 30-day online bioinformatics workshops.

How a Project Works

1
Project Scoping & Consultation: We discuss your research hypothesis, sequencing library design, biological replicates, and target deliverables.
2
Secure Data Ingestion & QC Audit: Raw FASTQ/BAM files are transferred via secure checksum-verified channels for pre-analysis quality control.
3
Formal Proposal & Milestone Agreement: You receive an itemized proposal detailing pipeline architecture, software versions, and delivery schedules.
4
Containerized Pipeline Execution: Analyses are executed using reproducible Nextflow or Snakemake pipelines containerized in Docker/Singularity.
5
Deliverables & Visual Reporting: You receive processed alignment files, annotated tables, publication-ready vector figures, and methods write-ups.
6
Post-Delivery Support & Review: We provide video walk-throughs and assist with responding to reviewer questions during paper submission.

Pipeline Development

Custom workflows built with Nextflow and Snakemake, tailored exactly to your specific lab protocols, NGS sequencing requirements, and computing environment.

Data Interpretation

Advanced statistical modeling and visualization to turn your VCFs and count matrices into actionable biological insights for research publication.

Consulting

Strategic advice on experimental design, sequencing depth requirements, and analytical strategy before you ever run a gel. Also explore our hands-on training workshops.

What We Deliver

Every research engagement concludes with clean, organized, and audit-ready computational assets. We do not provide opaque spreadsheets; our deliverables are engineered so that your laboratory can verify each step, archive intermediate data, and directly embed figures into scientific manuscripts:

  • Interactive MultiQC Quality Reports: Comprehensive HTML audits tracking read counts, base quality distributions, adapter contamination, and duplication metrics across all samples.
  • Coordinate-Sorted Alignments: Standardized BAM/CRAM files indexed with samtools, complete with alignment rate summaries and coverage metrics.
  • Processed Data Matrices & Annotations: Filtered expression count tables (DESeq2/edgeR ready) or functionally annotated VCF files with Ensembl VEP, SnpEff, or ClinVar annotations.
  • Publication-Grade Visualizations: High-resolution vector figures (PDF, TIFF, SVG) including PCA plots, volcano plots, clustered heatmaps, pathway enrichment bubble charts, and genomic track snapshots.
  • Manuscript-Ready Methodology: Fully articulated draft paragraphs for your manuscript's Materials & Methods section, specifying software release versions, reference genome builds, parameters, and citations.

Input Data We Accept

We accept raw sequencing outputs and intermediate files from academic sequencing cores and commercial facilities across India and internationally:

  • File Formats: Raw FASTQ files (gzipped), multiplexed sequencing runs, alignment BAM/CRAM files, raw variant call formats (VCF), and single-cell 10x Genomics feature-barcode matrices.
  • Sequencing Platforms: Short-read data from Illumina (NovaSeq, NextSeq, MiSeq) and MGI/DNBSEQ; long-read data from Oxford Nanopore Technologies (MinION, PromethION) and Pacific Biosciences (Sequel II, Revio HiFi).
  • Target Organisms: Human, mouse, rat, model agronomic crops (rice, wheat, Arabidopsis), livestock, fungal isolates, bacterial pathogens, and complex environmental microbial communities. Non-model organisms are supported with custom reference genome indexing.

Tools and Pipeline Frameworks

To guarantee reproducibility across computing clusters, our pipelines rely on containerized environments managed with Nextflow and Snakemake:

  • Quality Control & Trimming: FastQC, Fastp, Trimmomatic, MultiQC.
  • Sequence Alignment: STAR, HISAT2, BWA-MEM2, Bowtie2, Minimap2.
  • Transcriptomics & Quantification: featureCounts, Salmon, Kallisto, DESeq2, edgeR, clusterProfiler. Want to master transcriptomics yourself? See our RNA-Seq workshop and single-cell RNA-Seq workshop.
  • Epigenomics & Chromatin: MACS2/MACS3, deepTools, HOMER, DiffBind, ChIPseeker. Compare methods in our ChIP-Seq workshop and ATAC-Seq workshop.
  • Metagenomics: QIIME 2, DADA2, Kraken2, Bracken, MetaPhlAn, HUMAnN 3. Learn hands-on in our shotgun metagenomics workshop.
  • Workflow Management: Nextflow, Snakemake, Conda, Docker, and Singularity container configurations.

Frequently Asked Questions

What final deliverables do research clients receive upon project completion?

Every project includes comprehensive quality control audits (MultiQC), aligned coordinate-sorted BAM/CRAM files with indexes, processed count matrices or annotated VCFs, high-resolution publication-ready figures, and detailed methods write-ups citing software versions and parameters.

Which sequencing platforms and file formats does Xenoi Biotech accept?

We accept raw FASTQ or BAM files generated across Illumina, Oxford Nanopore Technologies (ONT), and Pacific Biosciences (PacBio) platforms, as well as downstream matrices, VCFs, or BED files for re-analysis.

Can you develop custom pipelines for non-model organisms or specialized assays?

Yes. We engineer bespoke Nextflow and Snakemake pipelines containerized in Docker or Singularity, tailored specifically for de novo assemblies, non-model organisms, or novel library protocols.

How is client research data protected and kept confidential?

We execute formal Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) prior to data ingestion, enforce encrypted transfer protocols, and maintain strict data segregation throughout the analytical lifecycle.

Can research scholars learn the analysis techniques used in their projects?

Yes. Xenoi Biotech operates 18 dedicated 30-day online bioinformatics workshops covering RNA-Seq, ChIP-Seq, ATAC-Seq, metagenomics, and variant calling for researchers seeking hands-on computational mastery.

BIOINFORMATICS & DATA ANALYSIS SERVICES

Bioinformatics Analysis Services & Pipelines

Xenoi Biotech provides end-to-end analysis of sequencing and array data, custom workflow engineering and statistical interpretation for academic researchers, biotechnology companies and clinical laboratories. Whether your project involves differential gene expression, chromatin accessibility, variant discovery or metagenomic profiling, we combine industry-standard tools with transparent reporting, so that your findings withstand peer review.

Based in Punjab, India, our team works with principal investigators and commercial teams to turn raw sequencing reads into validated biological insight, using documented and reproducible workflows. For focused sequencing projects, see our genomics analysis services. If you want to build these skills in your own lab, explore our 30-day online bioinformatics workshops.

How a project works

  1. 1Scoping and consultationWe discuss your hypothesis, library design, biological replicates and the deliverables you need.
  2. 2Data transfer and QCRaw FASTQ or BAM files arrive through checksum-verified channels for pre-analysis quality control.
  3. 3Proposal and milestonesYou receive an itemised proposal with the pipeline design, software versions and delivery schedule.
  4. 4Pipeline executionAnalyses run in reproducible Nextflow or Snakemake pipelines, containerised with Docker or Singularity.
  5. 5DeliverablesYou receive processed files, annotated tables, publication-ready figures and a methods write-up.
  6. 6Support and reviewWe provide video walk-throughs and help you respond to reviewer questions during submission.

What you receive, and what we need

What you receive

  • Interactive MultiQC reports covering read counts, base quality, adapter content and duplication
  • Coordinate-sorted, indexed BAM or CRAM alignments with alignment and coverage summaries
  • Filtered count tables (DESeq2 and edgeR ready) or annotated VCF files (Ensembl VEP, SnpEff, ClinVar)
  • Publication-grade vector figures: PCA, volcano plots, heatmaps, pathway enrichment and genome tracks
  • Draft Materials and Methods text with software versions, reference builds, parameters and citations

What we need from you

  • Raw FASTQ (gzipped), BAM or CRAM, VCF files, or 10x Genomics feature-barcode matrices
  • Short-read data from Illumina or MGI/DNBSEQ, or long-read data from Oxford Nanopore or PacBio
  • The organism and reference genome. Non-model organisms are supported with custom reference indexing
  • A sample sheet describing groups and replicates, and the question you want answered

Tools we use

Our pipelines use containerised environments managed with Nextflow and Snakemake, so results can be reproduced on other computing clusters.

  • QC and trimming FastQC · Fastp · Trimmomatic · MultiQC

Timelines, pricing and confidentiality

Turnaround

Duration depends on sample number, sequencing depth and the statistics required. Standard RNA-seq and WES cohorts typically take 5 to 15 business days once data integrity is confirmed. Send your sample numbers through the project inquiry form for an accurate estimate.

Pricing

Pricing is per sample or milestone-based for pipeline engineering. We send an itemised proposal within 24 working hours of receiving your project scope, with GST-compliant invoicing suitable for institutional grants and DST/DBT funding.

Confidentiality

We sign a mutual non-disclosure agreement before accepting proprietary samples. Data transfer uses TLS/SSL encryption, and project storage is partitioned and open only to the assigned analysts.

Frequently Asked Questions

What final deliverables do research clients receive upon project completion?

Every project includes comprehensive quality control audits (MultiQC), aligned coordinate-sorted BAM/CRAM files with indexes, processed count matrices or annotated VCFs, high-resolution publication-ready figures, and detailed methods write-ups citing software versions and parameters.

Which sequencing platforms and file formats does Xenoi Biotech accept?

We accept raw FASTQ or BAM files generated across Illumina, Oxford Nanopore Technologies (ONT), and Pacific Biosciences (PacBio) platforms, as well as downstream matrices, VCFs, or BED files for re-analysis.

Can you develop custom pipelines for non-model organisms or specialized assays?

Yes. We engineer bespoke Nextflow and Snakemake pipelines containerized in Docker or Singularity, tailored specifically for de novo assemblies, non-model organisms, or novel library protocols.

How is client research data protected and kept confidential?

We execute formal Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) prior to data ingestion, enforce encrypted transfer protocols, and maintain strict data segregation throughout the analytical lifecycle.

Can research scholars learn the analysis techniques used in their projects?

Yes. Xenoi Biotech operates 18 dedicated 30-day online bioinformatics workshops covering RNA-Seq, ChIP-Seq, ATAC-Seq, metagenomics, and variant calling for researchers seeking hands-on computational mastery.

Ready to discuss your bioinformatics project?

Submit your sequencing specifications to receive a formal scope and proposal within 24 working hours. Anonymized sample deliverable bundles are available on request.

Bioinformatics Services & Pipelines

From raw sequencing data to published discoveries, our comprehensive pipelines give you clarity and confidence in your genomic research.

This page provides an overview of Xenoi Biotech's bioinformatics analysis, custom pipeline development, data interpretation, and research consulting services. We develop reproducible computational workflows using Nextflow and Snakemake to turn sequencing datasets into publication-ready biological discoveries. Researchers and labs can explore specialized genomics analysis or request tailored proposals for genomic research support.

Pipeline Development

Custom workflows built with Nextflow and Snakemake, tailored exactly to your specific lab protocols, NGS sequencing requirements, and computing environment.

Data Interpretation

Advanced statistical modeling and visualization to turn your VCFs and count matrices into actionable biological insights for research publication.

Consulting

Strategic advice on experimental design, sequencing depth requirements, and analytical strategy before you ever run a gel. Also explore our hands-on training workshops.

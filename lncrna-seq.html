lncRNA-Seq 30-day workshop

Identify and study long non-coding RNAs from RNA-Seq data.

About this workshop

Long non-coding RNAs are transcripts longer than about 200 nucleotides that do not make proteins, yet many help to regulate genes, development and disease. They are hard to study because they are often expressed at low levels, vary between tissues and can look like coding transcripts at first sight. This workshop shows how to find them in RNA-Seq data: assembling transcripts, filtering candidates by length and exon structure, and testing coding potential with more than one predictor so the calls are trustworthy. You then measure their expression, compare conditions, and use co-expression and genomic position to suggest which genes a lncRNA may influence. The outcome is a defensible list of candidate lncRNAs and a plan for validating them in the lab.

Week 1

lncRNA biology What lncRNAs are and how they act.
Library preparation and design Choices that affect lncRNA detection.
Read quality control Clean reads before analysis.
Module 2 Alignment and assembly
Weeks 2-3
Mapping to the genome Align reads with a splice-aware tool.
Transcript assembly Reconstruct known and novel transcripts.
Merging across samples Build one unified transcript set.
Assembly review Compare against reference annotation.
Module 3 lncRNA identification
Weeks 2-3
Filtering candidates Apply length, exon and expression filters.
Coding-potential prediction Separate non-coding from protein-coding transcripts.
Combining evidence Use several predictors for a stronger call.
Classification Group lncRNAs by position relative to genes.
Module 4 Expression analysis
Weeks 3-4
Quantification Estimate lncRNA and mRNA abundance.
Differential expression Find lncRNAs that change between conditions.
Tissue and condition specificity Spot lncRNAs with restricted expression.
Quality checks Verify results with sample-level plots.
Module 5 Function prediction
Weeks 3-4
Co-expression networks Link lncRNAs to genes that move with them.
Cis and trans targets Predict nearby and distant regulated genes.
Enrichment of targets Infer likely biological roles.
Network visualization Present lncRNA-gene relationships.
Module 6 Mini-project and report
Week 4
Project dataset Run a full lncRNA study on public data.
Reproducible workflow Document each step and setting.
Figures and write-up Present candidates and predicted roles.
Review and next steps Plan validation experiments.

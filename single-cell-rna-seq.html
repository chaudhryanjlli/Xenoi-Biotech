TRANSCRIPTOMICS 30 days · 6 modules · Online

Single-cell RNA-Seq 30-day workshop

Explore cell types and states from single-cell expression data.

What you will learn

Module 1 Foundations
Week 1
Single-cell technologies How droplet and plate methods capture cells.
Data structure Cells, genes, barcodes and count matrices.
Setting up the environment Run R or Python tools for single-cell work.
Raw data processing Turn sequencing output into a cell-by-gene matrix.
Module 2 Quality control
Weeks 2-3
Cell filtering Remove empty droplets, damaged cells and doublets.
Gene filtering Keep informative genes.
Ambient RNA and artefacts Recognise and reduce background signal.
QC reports Summarise quality per sample.
Module 3 Normalization and structure
Weeks 2-3
Normalization Make cells comparable despite depth differences.
Variable genes Select genes that carry biological signal.
Dimensionality reduction PCA, UMAP and what they do and do not show.
Neighbour graphs Build the basis for clustering.
Module 4 Clusters and cell types
Weeks 3-4
Clustering Group similar cells and choose a resolution.
Marker genes Find genes that define each cluster.
Cell-type annotation Label clusters using markers and references.
Cluster stability Check that groups are real, not artefacts.
Module 5 Comparisons and trajectories
Weeks 3-4
Multi-sample integration Combine datasets and correct batch effects.
Differential expression between groups Compare conditions within cell types.
Trajectory analysis Order cells along development or response.
Cell communication basics Explore signalling between cell groups.
Module 6 Mini-project and report
Week 4
Project dataset Analyse a public single-cell dataset end to end.
Reproducible workflow Keep code, settings and versions together.
Figures and write-up Present clusters, markers and findings.
Review and next steps Discuss validation and follow-up experiments.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Single-cell RNA-Seq workshop online?

Yes. All Xenoi Biotech workshops are delivered online.

How long is it and how is it structured?

It runs for 30 days in 6 modules, from foundations to core analysis and interpretation, and ends with a mini-project on real data.

Who is it for?

Bachelor's, master's and PhD students, postdoctoral researchers and motivated independent learners.

What will I learn?

Explore cell types and states from single-cell expression data, covering six modules: Foundations, Quality control, Normalization and structure, Clusters and cell types, Comparisons and trajectories, Mini-project and report.

Bioinformatics & NGS Data Analysis Services and Training in India

Xenoi Biotech Pvt. Ltd. is a specialized bioinformatics company based in Punjab, India. We deliver end-to-end next-generation sequencing (NGS) data analysis services for academic and industry research laboratories, alongside eighteen intensive 30-day online bioinformatics workshops, student internships, and institutional collaborations across genomics, transcriptomics, epigenomics, and metagenomics.

| NGS & GENOMICS SERVICES

End-to-End Sequencing Data Analysis

From raw FASTQ reads to publication-grade figures, we support life-science researchers with rigorous statistical analysis and reproducible Nextflow and Snakemake pipelines. We specialize in Whole-Genome Sequencing (WGS), Whole-Exome Sequencing (WES), and custom bioinformatics pipeline development.

Whether identifying somatic variants in cancer genomics, profiling rare disease cohorts, or assembling non-model genomes, our analyses deliver coordinate-sorted indexed BAMs, filtered annotated VCFs, MultiQC audits, and draft methods for your manuscript.

| QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

What bioinformatics and NGS services does Xenoi Biotech provide?

Xenoi Biotech delivers end-to-end sequencing analysis services including Whole-Genome Sequencing (WGS), Whole-Exome Sequencing (WES), RNA-Seq differential expression, single-cell genomics, ChIP-Seq, ATAC-Seq, and metagenomic profiling using reproducible Nextflow pipelines.

How do Xenoi Biotech's 30-day online workshops differ from other short courses?

Unlike typical 3-to-4 day introductory webinars, each of our 18 workshops spans 30 days across six structured modules, providing guided hands-on execution on real research datasets from raw FASTQ to biological interpretation.

What specialized topics do the 18 bioinformatics workshops cover?

Workshops span five core domains: Transcriptomics (bulk, de novo, single-cell, spatial, lncRNA, miRNA, PSI-Seq), Epigenomics (ChIP-Seq, SNAP-ChIP, ATAC-Seq, single-cell ATAC-Seq, Methyl-Seq), Genomics (DNA-Seq, RAD-Seq, de novo assembly), Metagenomics (16S/ITS and shotgun), and Microarray analysis.

Are student bioinformatics internships available across India?

Yes. Our 4-week, 8-week, and 12-week virtual bioinformatics internships are open to undergraduate, postgraduate, and PhD scholars across India, with hands-on capstone project mentorship.

What is the fee for enrolling in a 30-day bioinformatics workshop?

Every 30-day online workshop fee is MRP ₹5,000, inclusive of GST. Batch dates are shared on enquiry.

Where is Xenoi Biotech located and how do we begin a project?

Xenoi Biotech Pvt. Ltd. is based in Punjab, India. To initiate a project or workshop registration, submit an inquiry via our contact page or email explore@xenoibiotech.com.

EPIGENOMICS 30 days · 6 modules · Online

ChIP-Seq 30-day workshop

Map where proteins and histone marks bind the genome, from raw reads to motifs.

What you will learn

Module 1 Foundations
Week 1
Linux and command-line basics Navigate files, run programs and keep analyses organised.
Sequencing and ChIP-Seq concepts How the assay works and what the reads represent.
File formats FASTQ, BAM, BED and bigWig, and when each is used.
Read quality control Judge read quality and trim adapters and poor bases.
Module 2 Alignment and filtering
Weeks 2-3
Genome indexing and mapping Align reads to a reference genome.
Filtering reads Remove duplicates, low-quality and blacklisted regions.
Library quality metrics Check enrichment and replicate agreement.
Signal tracks Create coverage files for genome-browser viewing.
Module 3 Peak calling
Weeks 2-3
Control and input samples Why a control matters and how it is used.
Narrow and broad peaks Choose settings for transcription factors vs histone marks.
Replicates Combine replicates into a reliable peak set.
Reviewing peaks Inspect calls visually and tune thresholds.
Module 4 Annotation and motifs
Weeks 3-4
Annotating peaks Link peaks to nearby genes and genomic features.
Motif discovery Detect the DNA patterns the protein recognises.
Functional enrichment Find pathways and processes behind bound genes.
Profile plots and heatmaps Show signal around peaks and gene starts.
Module 5 Differential binding
Weeks 3-4
Count matrices Quantify signal across a shared set of regions.
Statistical testing Identify gained and lost binding sites.
Comparing marks and conditions Overlap peak sets and interpret the differences.
Integrating expression Connect binding changes to gene activity.
Module 6 Mini-project and report
Week 4
Project dataset Work through a complete analysis on public data.
Reproducible workflow Record steps and settings so the work can be repeated.
Figures and report Prepare publication-style figures and a short write-up.
Review and next steps Discuss results and how to extend the analysis.

Frequently asked questions

Is the ChIP-Seq workshop online?

Yes. All Xenoi Biotech workshops are delivered online.

How long is it and how is it structured?

It runs for 30 days in 6 modules, from foundations to core analysis and interpretation, and ends with a mini-project on real data.

Who is it for?

Bachelor's, master's and PhD students, postdoctoral researchers and motivated independent learners.

What will I learn?

Map where proteins and histone marks bind the genome, from raw reads to motifs, covering six modules: Foundations, Alignment and filtering, Peak calling, Annotation and motifs, Differential binding, Mini-project and report.

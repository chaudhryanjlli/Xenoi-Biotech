About this workshop

RAD-Seq sequences only the DNA next to restriction-enzyme cut sites, which makes it a cost-effective way to find thousands of genetic markers across many individuals. That has made it popular for population genetics, linkage maps and studies of species with no genome. This workshop covers the whole analysis: understanding how the enzymes shape the data, separating reads by individual using barcodes, cleaning reads, and building loci with or without a reference genome. You then call SNPs, filter genotypes for depth, missing data and artefacts, and export the results for downstream tools. The final modules show how to measure diversity, visualise population structure with PCA and compare populations, so you can move from raw reads to a population-level result.

Frequently asked questions

Is this RAD-Seq workshop delivered online?

Yes. All Xenoi Biotech workshops are delivered 100% online, allowing researchers and students across India and internationally to participate with remote compute support.

How long is the workshop and what is the workload?

The workshop runs for 30 days across 6 structured modules. Learners typically dedicate 1 to 2 flexible hours per day to work through pipeline commands, code exercises, and the mini-project.

Who is this workshop designed for?

It is built for B.Sc., M.Sc., and Ph.D. students, postdoctoral researchers, faculty members, and industry professionals in biotechnology, biochemistry, microbiology, and medicine who need to analyze NGS datasets independently.

What will I learn in this workshop?

You will learn Stacks pipeline execution (process_radtags, ustacks/pstacks, cstacks, sstacks, populations), SNP filtering for call rate and minor allele frequency, Fst differentiation, heterozygosity metrics, PCA, and ancestry clustering.

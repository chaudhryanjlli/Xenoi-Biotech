GENOMICS 30 days · 6 modules · Online

RAD-Seq 30-day workshop

Discover genetic markers in many individuals using reduced-representation sequencing.

What you will learn

Module 1 Foundations
Week 1
Linux and command-line basics Run tools and manage files.
RAD-Seq principle How restriction enzymes reduce the genome.
Experimental design Enzymes, sample numbers and depth.
Read quality control Check and clean reads.
Module 2 Demultiplexing and cleaning
Weeks 2-3
Barcodes Separate reads by individual.
Trimming and filtering Remove low-quality and short reads.
Recovering reads Handle errors in barcodes and cut sites.
Sample summary Report reads per individual.
Module 3 Locus construction
Weeks 2-3
With a reference genome Align reads and build loci.
Without a reference genome Cluster reads into de novo loci.
Parameter choices Understand settings that change locus numbers.
Locus quality Check coverage and missing data.
Module 4 SNP discovery
Weeks 3-4
Calling SNPs Identify variants across individuals.
Genotype filtering Apply depth, missing-data and frequency filters.
Removing paralogs and artefacts Reduce false variants.
Output formats Export data for downstream tools.
Module 5 Population analysis
Weeks 3-4
Diversity measures Compute heterozygosity and related statistics.
Population structure Use PCA and clustering to see groups.
Differentiation Compare populations with FST.
Visualization Plot results for presentation.
Module 6 Mini-project and report
Week 4
Project dataset Analyse a public RAD-Seq dataset.
Reproducible workflow Record every step and setting.
Figures and write-up Present structure and diversity results.
Review and next steps Discuss design improvements.

Frequently asked questions

Is the RAD-Seq workshop online?

Yes. All Xenoi Biotech workshops are delivered online.

How long is it and how is it structured?

It runs for 30 days in 6 modules, from foundations to core analysis and interpretation, and ends with a mini-project on real data.

Who is it for?

Bachelor's, master's and PhD students, postdoctoral researchers and motivated independent learners.

What will I learn?

Discover genetic markers in many individuals using reduced-representation sequencing, covering six modules: Foundations, Demultiplexing and cleaning, Locus construction, SNP discovery, Population analysis, Mini-project and report.

Collaboration with Colleges & Institutions

Bridging academic life sciences with real-world computational biology through customized curriculum workshops, faculty development programmes, research MoUs, and student internships across India.

Modern biological discovery is fundamentally data-driven. However, conventional university curricula often face gaps in high-throughput sequencing data analysis, Linux server computing, and statistical programming. Based in Punjab, India, Xenoi Biotech collaborates with colleges, universities, and research institutes across India to empower life-science departments. Through structured academic partnerships, we provide students with hands-on exposure to production-grade Nextflow workflows, cloud computing environments, and real experimental sequencing datasets. Explore our 18 online bioinformatics workshops and research internships designed to complement degree studies.

What Partner Colleges & Institutions Receive

Career Awareness & Technical Seminars

Interactive lectures introducing undergraduates and postgraduates to modern computational biology, omics industry career pathways, and essential data science skills.

On-Campus Bootcamps & Online Workshops

Customised intensive training sessions and 30-day cohorts in RNA-Seq, DNA-Seq, and metagenomics tailored to departmental syllabi.

Dedicated Internship Placements

Guaranteed project intake for 4-week, 8-week, or 12-week virtual bioinformatics internships with individual capstone mentorship.

Faculty Development Programmes (FDP)

Hands-on upskilling modules for life science faculty covering Nextflow pipelines, R Bioconductor packages, and reproducible bioinformatics teaching workflows.

How to Start: The Institutional MoU Process

Establishing an academic Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with Xenoi Biotech is straightforward and transparent:

1
Preliminary Departmental Consultation: An initial discussion with your Dean, Head of Department (HOD), or faculty coordinators to identify student cohort needs and curriculum goals.
2
Drafting Scope of Collaboration: We prepare a customized framework outlining seminar topics, hands-on workshop schedules, student fee concessions, and faculty training scopes.
3
Institutional Review & Formal Signing: Mutual signing of the non-financial MoU, creating formal documentation for NAAC accreditation and university industry-liaison compliance.
4
Programme Rollout & Student Onboarding: Execution of scheduled webinars, hands-on software labs, and priority allocation of research internship seats.

Frequently Asked Questions

What institutional benefits do colleges receive by partnering with Xenoi Biotech?

Partner institutions gain curriculum-aligned bioinformatics seminars, dedicated faculty development programs (FDPs), subsidized hands-on NGS workshops, and priority internship placements for life-sciences students.

How does an institution initiate a formal Memorandum of Understanding (MoU)?

The MoU process begins with an exploratory meeting between Xenoi Biotech and department heads or deans, followed by mutual drafting of scope and deliverables, institutional review, and signing with zero upfront mandatory financial overhead.

Can training sessions and workshops be conducted on-campus?

Yes. While our core 30-day technical workshops run online for flexible computational scheduling, we conduct 1-to-3 day intensive on-campus seminars and hands-on bootcamps at partner campuses across India.

Are internships and workshops aligned with NAAC and NIRF accreditation criteria?

Yes. Our collaborative programs provide structured industry-academia linkage documentation, student training certificates, and project evaluation reports supporting NAAC Criterion 3 and NIRF parameters.

Collaboration with Colleges & Institutions

Bridging academic life sciences with real-world computational biology through customized curriculum workshops, faculty development programmes, research MoUs, and student internships across India.

Modern biological discovery is fundamentally data-driven. However, conventional university curricula often face gaps in high-throughput sequencing data analysis, Linux server computing, and statistical programming. Based in Punjab, India, Xenoibiotech collaborates with colleges, universities, and research institutes across India to empower life-science departments. Through structured academic partnerships, we provide students with hands-on exposure to production-grade Nextflow workflows, cloud computing environments, and real experimental sequencing datasets. Explore our 18 online bioinformatics workshops and research internships designed to complement degree studies.

How to Start: The Institutional MoU Process

Establishing an academic Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with Xenoi Biotech is straightforward and transparent:

1
Preliminary Departmental Consultation: An initial discussion with your Dean, Head of Department (HOD), or faculty coordinators to identify student cohort needs and curriculum goals.
2
Drafting Scope of Collaboration: We prepare a customized framework outlining seminar topics, hands-on workshop schedules, student fee concessions, and faculty training scopes.
3
Institutional Review & Formal Signing: Mutual signing of the non-financial MoU, creating formal documentation for NAAC accreditation and university industry-liaison compliance.
4
Programme Rollout & Student Onboarding: Execution of scheduled webinars, hands-on software labs, and priority allocation of research internship/training seats.

DNA-Seq (variant analysis) 30-day workshop

Find and interpret DNA variants from sequencing data.

About this workshop

Sequencing a genome or exome produces millions of short reads, and the real work is turning them into a short, trustworthy list of variants that could matter. This workshop follows that path: cleaning reads, aligning them to a reference, preparing the alignments, and calling single-nucleotide variants and small insertions and deletions. You learn how quality scores and filters separate real variants from errors, and how annotation predicts the effect of each change and shows how common it is in populations. You then narrow the list using inheritance patterns and gene relevance, and check key variants visually in a genome browser. The mini-project applies the full pipeline to a real exome or genome dataset, so you finish with a method you can reuse.

What you will learn

Module 1 Foundations
Week 1
Linux and command-line basics Run tools and organise projects.
DNA sequencing designs Whole-genome, exome and targeted panels compared.
File formats FASTQ, BAM, VCF and reference files.
Read quality control Clean reads before alignment.
Module 2 Alignment
Weeks 2-3
Reference genome preparation Index the genome and annotation files.
Mapping reads Align reads to the reference.
Post-alignment processing Sort, mark duplicates and recalibrate.
Coverage and quality metrics Check depth and mapping performance.
Module 3 Variant calling
Weeks 2-3
SNPs and indels Call small variants from alignments.
Calling strategies Single-sample and multi-sample approaches.
Hard filters and quality scores Remove unreliable calls.
Comparing callers See how different tools agree.
Module 4 Annotation
Weeks 3-4
Effect prediction Classify variants by their predicted impact.
Population frequency Separate common from rare variants.
Known variant databases Check clinical and research resources.
Annotated tables Build files ready to filter.
Module 5 Filtering and prioritization
Weeks 3-4
Filtering strategies Narrow thousands of variants to a short list.
Inheritance patterns Apply family and case-control logic.
Candidate genes Rank variants by likely relevance.
Visual review Confirm important variants in a genome browser.
Module 6 Mini-project and report
Week 4
Project dataset Analyse a public exome or genome dataset.
Reproducible workflow Record every step and setting.
Figures and write-up Summarize variants and candidates.
Review and next steps Discuss validation and limitations.

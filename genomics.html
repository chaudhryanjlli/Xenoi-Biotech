NGS & GENOMICS

Genomics & NGS Data Analysis

Specialized Next-Generation Sequencing data analysis services for whole genomes, exomes, targeted panels, and de novo assemblies, designed for high-impact research publications.

Our analysis services are for research use unless agreed otherwise in writing.

High-throughput sequencing produces immense datasets where identifying authentic biological variants demands rigorous error-filtering, standardized alignment algorithms, and multi-database functional annotation. Based in Punjab, India, Xenoi Biotech assists academic institutions, clinical genetics researchers, and agro-biotechnology teams across India and globally. We process short-read and long-read sequencing data across model and non-model systems, bridging raw instrument outputs with actionable biological interpretation. In addition to sequencing analysis, explore our broader bioinformatics pipeline services, or build computational competence through our DNA-Seq workshop and genome assembly workshop.

How a Genomics Project Works

1
Sequencing Specifications Review: We examine your assay design, target depth (e.g. 30x WGS, 100x WES), platform chemistry, and reference assembly requirements.
2
Checksum Data Validation: Raw FASTQ files are transferred via secure checksum-verified channels to confirm read integrity and lack of file truncation.
3
Pipeline Configuration: Workflows are parameterized according to GATK Best Practices or DeepVariant pipelines containerized in Nextflow.
4
Variant Calling & Multi-Tier Filtering: High-confidence SNVs, indels, CNVs, and SVs are detected, filtered, and annotated against public databases.
5
Deliverable Generation: We deliver coordinate-sorted indexed BAM/CRAM files, annotated VCFs/TSVs, MultiQC audits, and circos/circos-style plots.
6
Post-Delivery Consultation: Dedicated scientific consultation to guide interpretation of candidate variants and assist in drafting manuscript methods.

Whole-Genome Sequencing (WGS)

High-precision variant calling (SNPs, Indels, SVs, CNVs) across complete genomes for population genomics and trait mapping.

Whole-Exome Sequencing (WES)

Deep coverage analysis of coding regions for rare disease etiology, oncological somatic mutation calling, and pedigree analysis.

Targeted Panels

Focused sequencing analysis for custom amplicon or hybridization capture panels with ultra-deep coverage verification.

De Novo Assembly

De novo assembly, scaffolding, and structural annotation of novel microbial, plant, and fungal genomes using hybrid sequencing strategies.

What We Deliver

Genomics datasets require standardized data formats to ensure reproducibility and seamless integration into downstream statistical analyses or medical genetics archives. For each sample cohort, we deliver:

  • Sequencing QC Audits: MultiQC aggregation evaluating base call quality per cycle, GC distribution, duplication levels, and target capture efficiency (for WES/panels).
  • Analysis-Ready Alignments: Coordinate-sorted BAM or compressed CRAM files with duplicate read markings (Picard/GATK MarkDuplicates) and BAI/CRAI index files.
  • Calibrated Variant Call Sets: High-confidence VCF files containing filtered SNVs and small indels, provided with both site-only and sample-level genotype quality fields.
  • Deep Functional Annotations: Formatted variant spreadsheets annotated via Ensembl Variant Effect Predictor (VEP) and SnpEff, incorporating allele frequencies from gnomAD, clinical significance from ClinVar, and pathogenicity predictions (REVEL, CADD).
  • Structural Variation & CNV Tables: Tabulated copy number alterations and structural rearrangement calls (inversions, duplications, translocations) with breakpoint coordinates.
  • Publication Visualizations & Methods: Visual summaries (circos plots, variant density distribution charts) and complete draft methodology write-ups for journal submission.

Input Data We Accept

Our computational pipelines accommodate data from major sequencing platforms, core facilities, and collaborative consortia:

  • Primary Raw Data: Paired-end or single-end FASTQ files (compressed .fastq.gz), multiplexed sequencing archives, and unaligned BAM (uBAM) files.
  • Pre-Aligned Data: BAM or CRAM files for variant recalibration, re-annotation, or joint genotyping cohorts.
  • Platforms Supported: Illumina platforms (NovaSeq 6000, NovaSeq X Plus, NextSeq 2000, MiSeq), MGI/DNBSEQ (G400, T7), Oxford Nanopore Technologies (R10.4.1 flow cells), and PacBio (Revio, Sequel IIe HiFi reads).
  • Genomic Systems: Human (GRCh37/hg19, GRCh38/hg38, T2T-CHM13), model vertebrates, crop species, agricultural pathogens, and environmental isolates.

Tools, Callers, and Pipelines

All genomic analyses are executed using benchmarked software implementations adhering to GATK Best Practices, deep learning variant models, and community-validated pipelines:

  • Alignment & Preprocessing: BWA-MEM2, Bowtie2, Minimap2, samtools, Picard Tools, fastp.
  • Variant Calling: GATK HaplotypeCaller, Google DeepVariant, FreeBayes, Strelka2, Mutect2 (somatic), Clair3 (long-read). Interested in variant calling? Enrol in our DNA-Seq data analysis workshop.
  • Structural Variation & CNV: Manta, Delly, LUMPY, CNVkit, Control-FREEC, Sniffles2.
  • De Novo Assembly & Scaffolding: SPAdes, Flye, Hifiasm, Canu, RagTag. Learn assembly hands-on in our whole-genome assembly workshop.
  • Annotation Engines: Ensembl VEP, SnpEff, ANNOVAR, ClinVar, dbSNP, gnomAD, COSMIC.
  • Population & Epigenetics: PLINK, VCFtools, Bismark. Explore epigenetic variation in our Methyl-Seq workshop.

Frequently Asked Questions

What variant types are identified in Whole-Genome and Exome Sequencing analysis?

Our pipelines detect single nucleotide variants (SNVs), small insertions/deletions (Indels), copy number variants (CNVs), and large structural variants (SVs) including inversions and translocations using validated algorithms like GATK HaplotypeCaller, DeepVariant, and Manta.

Which reference genomes and annotation databases are utilized?

We utilize standard reference builds such as GRCh38/hg38 and T2T-CHM13 for human studies, alongside organism-specific Ensembl and NCBI builds. Functional annotation is performed using Ensembl VEP, SnpEff, ClinVar, gnomAD, and dbSNP.

Can you process long-read sequencing data from Oxford Nanopore or PacBio?

Yes. We operate dedicated workflows for long-read data, including PacBio HiFi reads and Oxford Nanopore FASTQ/BAM files, using specialized tools such as Minimap2, Clair3, Flye, and Medaka for accurate assembly and SV discovery.

Are your genomics analysis services intended for clinical diagnosis?

Unless specifically contracted under clinical compliance frameworks in writing, all sequencing analysis deliverables provided by Xenoibiotech are strictly intended for research use only (RUO).

How can researchers learn hands-on variant calling and assembly pipelines?

Researchers and scholars can enrol in our 30-day online workshops, including DNA-Seq Variant Analysis, Whole-Genome De Novo Assembly, and RAD-Seq, each priced at MRP ₹5,000 (incl. GST).
